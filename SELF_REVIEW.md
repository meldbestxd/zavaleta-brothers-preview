# Self Review and Improvements

## v1.0

Built a polished two-page contractor website with:

- Homepage with hero, services, gallery preview, about, service area, and contact.
- Dedicated gallery page with filterable project cards.
- Clickable phone, email, Facebook, and save-contact actions.
- SEO title and meta descriptions.
- Source notes and curated assets.

## Review perspectives

### 1. Homeowner

The first version explained services clearly, but the homepage hero was too wordy and the business-card image in the estimate panel felt less premium than the rest of the design.

Improvement made in v1.1:

- Shortened hero headline to “Asphalt that looks sharp and lasts longer.”
- Reworked the estimate card into a clean customer-focused pricing panel.
- Kept phone and email easy to tap.

### 2. Professional web designer

The visual system was strong: blacktop-inspired palette, yellow accent, large typography, rounded cards, and strong contrast. The biggest issue was reducing visual noise in the hero.

Improvement made in v1.1:

- Removed the low-quality brand card from the hero panel.
- Added simple service chips inside the quote panel.
- Preserved the asphalt project image as the visual anchor.

### 3. Local SEO specialist

The site had basic SEO and local service wording, but it needed structured business data and more question-based service content.

Improvements made:

- v1.1 added LocalBusiness JSON-LD with phone, email, hours, area served, website, and Facebook.
- v1.2 added FAQ-style service content about seal coating, crack sealing, line striping, and estimate requests.

### 4. Mobile user

Mobile layout worked well with strong tap targets, readable text, and stacked content. The hero headline became much easier to scan after v1.1.

Improvement made in v1.2:

- Ensured FAQ cards collapse cleanly on tablet and mobile.

### 5. Business owner seeking leads

The site needed to quickly turn visitors into calls or estimate requests. The phone CTA was strong, but estimate instructions could be clearer.

Improvements made:

- Added “Free Estimates” badge in hero card.
- Added direct instructions for what to send: property type, address/city, photos, service needed, size, and timeline.
- Added Facebook CTAs without making the website depend on Facebook loading.

## Final version

Current final local version: v1.2.

No deployment was performed.
