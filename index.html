Helpful Answers

Questions property owners ask before asphalt work.

These answers help customers understand when to request seal coating, crack sealing, asphalt repair, or striping.

When should asphalt be seal coated?

When it looks faded, dry, or worn but still has a solid base. Seal coating improves appearance and helps protect the surface.

Why seal cracks before winter?

Open cracks let water in. Cold weather can freeze and expand that water, making damage worse over time.

Can lines be refreshed after seal coating?

Yes. Fresh striping after seal coating gives commercial lots a sharper look and makes spaces easier to see.

What should I send for an estimate?
